Our Story

Born from a Love of
Good Coffee & Better Mornings

Brew & Bean started in 2014 as a tiny 12-seat counter on Market Street. Two baristas, one espresso machine, and a belief that the best part of anyone's day deserves to be savoured.

Today we've grown — but not grown up. Every bean is sourced directly from farms we visit. Every recipe was born from curiosity, not convention.
